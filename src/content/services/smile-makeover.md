## What it means

A smile makeover is not a single procedure. It is a treatment plan that
combines several treatments to address the appearance of the teeth that show
when you smile, sequenced so they work together.

Depending on what you want changed, a plan might draw on
[whitening](/services/teeth-whitening/),
[veneers](/services/veneers/),
[tooth-coloured fillings](/services/tooth-coloured-fillings/),
[crowns](/services/dental-crowns/),
[orthodontics](/services/orthodontics-braces/),
or replacing missing teeth with
[implants](/services/dental-implants/) or [bridges](/services/dental-bridges/).

## Why plan it as a whole

Order matters. Whitening is done before veneers or crowns are made, because
those materials will not change shade afterwards and are matched to the teeth
around them. Alignment is usually corrected before anything is bonded to the
front of a tooth, so less enamel needs removing. Doing treatments in the wrong
sequence can mean redoing work.

## Health first

Any active decay or gum disease is treated before cosmetic work begins.
Appearance-focused treatment placed over an untreated problem stores up
failure — a veneer on a tooth with decay beneath it is not a solved problem.

## How the planning works

1. **Examination and discussion.** What you want changed, in your words.
2. **Records.** Photographs, radiographs, and impressions or a scan.
3. **A plan with options.** Usually more than one route, with the trade-offs,
   duration and cost of each set out.
4. **A preview where possible.** A wax-up or digital mock-up so you can see the
   proposed result before irreversible work begins.
5. **Treatment in sequence.**

## Honest expectations

Results vary between individuals. What is achievable depends on your starting
point — the position of the teeth, the health of the gums and supporting bone,
how much natural tooth structure remains, and the shape of your bite. Some
components are irreversible, particularly where enamel is removed for veneers
or crowns.

The purpose of the planning stage is that you understand all of that, and can
see a preview of the outcome, before committing to anything permanent.

## Call the clinic first

If you are in pain or have had an injury, phone before travelling so the team can
advise you and make time for you.

**Seek immediate medical help at a hospital** — not a dental clinic — if there is
swelling that affects your breathing, swallowing or vision, or swelling spreading
to the eye or neck, or if you have a high fever with facial swelling.

## Common emergencies

**Knocked-out permanent tooth.** Time matters. Hold the tooth by the crown, not
the root. If it is dirty, rinse briefly with milk or saline — not water, and do
not scrub. If you can, place it back in the socket and bite gently on a cloth.
Otherwise keep it in milk and come straight in. Do not attempt to reimplant a
baby tooth.

**Broken or chipped tooth.** Save any fragments in milk. Rinse with warm water.
Cover a sharp edge with sugar-free gum if it is cutting your tongue.

**Severe toothache.** Ordinary pain relief taken as directed on the packet may
help until you are seen. Do not place aspirin against the gum — it burns the
tissue.

**Bleeding after an extraction.** Roll clean gauze into a pad, place it over the
socket and bite firmly for 30 minutes without checking. Repeat once if needed. If
bleeding continues, call the clinic.

**Lost filling or crown.** Keep the crown. Avoid chewing on that side and arrange
an appointment.

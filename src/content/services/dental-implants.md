## What it is

An implant is a small titanium post placed into the jawbone, which acts as an
artificial tooth root. Once the bone has integrated with it, a crown is attached
on top.

## Who it suits

Implants need enough healthy bone to hold them and healthy gums around them.
Smoking, uncontrolled diabetes and some medications affect healing and may make
another option more suitable. An examination and a scan determine whether an
implant is appropriate for you.

## The stages

1. **Assessment** — examination, X-rays or a 3D scan, and a treatment plan.
2. **Placement** — the post is placed under local anaesthetic.
3. **Healing** — bone integrates with the implant, typically over three to six
   months.
4. **Restoration** — an abutment and crown are fitted.

## Afterwards

Implants are cleaned like natural teeth, with the addition of interdental
brushes around the post. They do not decay, but the gum and bone around them can
become inflamed if plaque builds up, so regular check-ups continue to matter.

## Alternatives

A bridge uses the neighbouring teeth for support and is quicker, but involves
preparing those teeth. A removable partial denture is the least invasive and
least expensive option. Each has trade-offs the dentist will go through with you.

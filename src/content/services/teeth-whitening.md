## What it is

Whitening uses a peroxide-based gel to lighten the natural shade of tooth
enamel. It is carried out either in the clinic in a single longer appointment,
or at home with custom-made trays over about two weeks.

## What it can and cannot change

Whitening works on natural tooth structure. It does not change the colour of
crowns, veneers or fillings — these stay the shade they were made, which can
leave them looking darker or lighter than the surrounding teeth afterwards.
Where discolouration comes from within a tooth rather than the surface, other
approaches may be needed.

Results vary between individuals, depending on the starting shade and the cause
of discolouration.

## Before treatment

An examination first, because whitening over untreated decay or exposed roots
causes unnecessary sensitivity, and because it helps to know where any existing
restorations sit.

## Afterwards

Temporary sensitivity to cold is common and settles within a few days. Avoid
strongly staining food and drink for the first 48 hours. The effect fades
gradually over months to years depending on diet and smoking.
